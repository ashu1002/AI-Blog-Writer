# Voice & Style Preferences

Learned from user feedback on 2026-08-25. Auto-loaded by blog-write, blog-rewrite,
blog-brief, blog-outline, blog-calendar, blog-strategy per the blog orchestrator's
project-root context rules.

## Tone
- Conversational, punchy, curiosity-driven. Hook the reader early, make them want
  to keep scrolling. Entertain, don't lecture.
- Use rhetorical questions, mini cliffhangers, and plain-language framing over
  dry technical recaps.
- Casual but credible. Confident, a little cheeky, never stiff or corporate.

## Hard rules
- **Never use em-dashes (—) anywhere in blog content.** Use commas, periods,
  parentheses, or colons instead. Rewrite the sentence if needed rather than
  reach for an em-dash.
- Always include long-tail keyword phrases naturally in the title, tags, and
  subheadings (not just the primary keyword).
- Always open with a **Table of Contents** (linked to each H2) right after the
  Key Takeaways box, for every post over ~1,000 words.
- Always include a **FAQ section with 6-8 questions**, phrased the way real
  people actually search ("is X safe", "how much does X cost", "did X pay
  ransom"), not just 2-3 generic ones.
- Prioritize reading-friendliness: short paragraphs (2-4 sentences), frequent
  subheads, bullets/bolded lead-ins where useful, no dense text walls.
